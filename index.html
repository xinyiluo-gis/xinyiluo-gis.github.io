Short Bio

I am an undergraduate student in Geographic Information Science at Sichuan Agricultural University, with a GPA of 4.46/5.0 and a ranking of 1/77. I have been admitted to the master's program in Pattern Recognition and Intelligent Systems (081104) at the School of Remote Sensing and Information Engineering, Wuhan University.

My research combines satellite image time series, state-space models, explainable machine learning, and spatial analysis. I am particularly interested in methods that remain reliable when observations are irregular, incomplete, or spatially heterogeneous.
